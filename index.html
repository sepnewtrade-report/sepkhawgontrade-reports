  • Gross Proceeds = Sell Price × Shares
  • Gross P/L = Gross Proceeds − Investment Cost
  • Total Fees = (Buy Value + Sell Value) × Commission % + Other Fees
  • Net P/L = Gross P/L − Total Fees
  • Return % = (Net P/L ÷ Investment Cost) × 100
